3.2 Vocabulary
FPY First Pass Yield: the number of units successfully produced in a process step, i.e., without
defects, divided by the total number of units entering that process.
TPY / FTY First Time Yield: is a calculation used to determine when the total number of acceptable
product leaves the process when rework/scrap are counted, but only if the rework performed
corrects the defect(s).
DPMO Defects per million of opportunity.
This indicator used to bench a process.
This indicator can be computed at any step of a SMT line or for the full process.
On Pi, it is calculated as the number of defect divided by the number of stencil aperture of the
panel multiply by 1 million.
JEDEC JEDEC is an acronym for the Joint Electronic Devices Engineering Council. It is an international
body of semiconductor manufacturers that set integrated circuit (IC) standards. JEDEC was
originally created in 1960 to cover the standardization of discrete semiconductor devices and
later expanded in 1970 to include integrated circuits. JEDEC does its work through its 50
committees/subcommittees that are overseen by the JEDEC Board of Directors. Member
companies in JEDEC include both manufacturers and users of semiconductor components as
well as others who are allied to the field.
For Vi TECHNOLOGY, JEDEC is a unique identifier for component outline (no functional
properties just geometric properties). A JEDEC can linked to many Part number.
Part Number Part Number: unique identifier in our customer information system.
This reference can be inherited from an ERP.

4.1.5 Stencil offset chart
The chart is a two dimensional chart centered on 0,0. Units in the
two axis are micrometers. The scale both axis must be equal
(aspect ratio 1:1)
For each panel of the production, the stencil offset is displayed
as a dot in the chart:
Dot color:
green color for the "back" squeegee direction panel
blue color for the "forth"
Dot opacity change with age of the panel:
latest is 100%
oldest is 40%
4.1.6 Volume per panel
This chart represents a curve of average paste volume of each
panel. panel are time ordered on the X axis
For each point of the curve,
a upper bar will represent the quantile 75
a lower bar will represent the quantile 25
